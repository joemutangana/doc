Idioms, meanings and example sentences:

1. Break the ice
Meaning: To start a conversation in a social situation.
Sentence: She told a funny joke to break the ice at the meeting.

2. Piece of cake
Meaning: Something very easy.
Sentence: The math test was a piece of cake.

3. Once in a blue moon
Meaning: Very rarely.
Sentence: He visits his grandparents once in a blue moon.

4. Hit the nail on the head
Meaning: To say something exactly right.
Sentence: You hit the nail on the head with your answer.

5. Spill the beans
Meaning: To reveal a secret.
Sentence: She accidentally spilled the beans about the surprise party.
