Roll a die of 6 sides, if you roll 1 you win $10, if 2 or 3 you win $5 if you roll 6 you lose $2, othe outcome nothing you win. how much is a total win? (3pts)

S={1,2,3,4,5,6}

let P(A) be to get 1=1/6 worth $10
let P(B) be to get 2=1/6 worth $5
let P(C) be to get 3=1/6 worth $5
let P(D) be to get 6=1/6 worth -$2
let P(E) be to get 2 or 3 worth which is 1/6 + 1/6 = P(B) + P(C) = 2/6 , $5+$5 = $10

Total lose is 1/6 = -$2
Total win will be P(E) * P(A) = 10 * 10 = $100
